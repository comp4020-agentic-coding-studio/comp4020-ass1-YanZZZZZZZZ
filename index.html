Every glyph on this page is a programmatically drawn stylized impression, not a scan or rubbing of a real artifact. For the Oracle Bone, Bronze, and Seal Script eras, the shape is a simplified line reconstruction based on each character's well-documented early form (its known strokes, redrawn as vector line art); for the Clerical, Regular, and Simplified eras — whose shapes are already close to today's printed character — the real Unicode glyph is rendered directly, with era-appropriate roughness or flattening layered on top. Each era's region is one representative place associated with that script, not a specific excavation site for any single character.
